Descriptions

Short Description

A Queens-style logic puzzle with a cosy pet companion. Play fully offline.

Long Description

Claw Spot is a calm, offline-first Queens-style logic grid puzzle for Android, paired with a cosy virtual pet waiting in the nest. Designed by solo developer Kim Lung Wong at Minsky Games Workshop in Nottingham, UK, it offers 1,000 handcrafted campaign levels that scale from quick 5×5 tutorials up to 12×12 expert boards.

The rules are simple to grasp and deeply satisfying to deduce: place exactly one claw in every row, every column, and every coloured region, with no two claws touching—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. There are no countdown timers rushing the player and no guessing required. Tap a cell to cycle through ✕ (blocked) and ? (maybe), hold to commit a claw, or enable optional auto-marking in Settings so blocked cells cross out automatically.

Between puzzles, players visit the nest to care for their chosen cat or dog by feeding, playing, or sharing a snack. Keeping the companion happy increases the nest level and charges Pet Power, which lets players place temporary draft claws on tricky boards without risking a heart. Earning new best star ratings awards coins that unlock hint charges or the warm dusk Night board theme in the Shop.

Key Features

  • 1,000 campaign puzzles: Progress from 5×5 Tutorial grids through Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert boards up to 12×12.
  • Pure deduction, zero timers: Every board is solvable by logic alone, with tap-to-mark (✕ / ?), hold-to-place, optional auto-mark, Undo, and Restart.
  • Cosy cat or dog companion: Care for a cat or dog in the nest; happiness levels up the nest and fuels Pet Power for risk-free draft claws.
  • Stars, coins & Night board: Earn up to 3 stars per puzzle, collect coins on new best clears (plus an optional daily Shop ad), and unlock the warm dusk Night board theme.
  • 100% offline & private: No account, no login, and local-only save data—playable anywhere on flights or commutes.
  • 16 languages built in: Instant language switching in Settings across 16 supported languages, with quiet piano music and optional haptics.
